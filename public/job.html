Job: the test

A reference card from Session 1. Details checked against the Hebrew, because the feast scene is easy to misremember.

Weekly readingStory mapJob

Where this sits

Job is among the oldest voices in Scripture. The adversary is already in the story, and the sons of God are already a council. That is why we opened it in Week 1.

The council scene

Sons of God present themselves before Yahweh
Job 1:6-12. The adversary is among them. He does not sneak in. He is there.
The hedge and the permission
Yahweh points to Job. The adversary says Job only fears God because of the hedge. Yahweh allows the test. Life is withheld from the adversary. He cannot kill Job.

What actually fell

Oxen, donkeys, sheep, camels, servants
Wave after wave of loss. Job tears his robe, shaves, worships. He does not charge Yahweh with wrong. Job 1:13-22.
The children
Not a wedding. They were eating and drinking wine in the house of their eldest brother. A great wind came from the wilderness. The house fell on the young people. They died. Job 1:18-19.
Skin and ash
Second permission: bone and flesh, not life. Sores. The ash heap. His wife. Then the friends. Job 2:1-10.

The whirlwind

Yahweh answers from the storm. Where were you when I laid the earth? Morning stars sang. Sons of God shouted. Job 38:4-7. That is the verse that puts the heavenly family at creation.

Why we read it in Week 1

The adversary tests. Yahweh remains God. Permission is not absence. The council is not a cartoon. And the sons of God were already there when the world was made.
