Theological Foundations

The Five Canonical Pillars

Our non-negotiable boundaries ensuring absolute fidelity to historic biblical orthodoxy and the primacy of Jesus Christ.

01

YHWH Alone is Sovereign

There is only one uncreated Creator: יהוה (YHWH). We never replace His sacred covenant name with generic cultural titles.

02

Jesus Christ is the Singular Seed

Yeshua HaMashiach is the incarnate Word, the Davidic King, who disarmed the powers at the Cross and rose bodily from the grave.

03

The Divine Council & Nations Reclaimed

Acknowledging God's heavenly assembly (Psalm 82) and the reversal of the Babel disinheritance across all 70 nations at Pentecost.

04

Living Temple of the Holy Spirit

The Parakletos takes up residence in ordinary believers, writing God's covenant love and righteousness directly onto human hearts.

05

Manuscript Integrity

Grounding every insight directly in the Hebrew Masoretic Text, Greek Septuagint, and Dead Sea Scrolls without speculative distortion.
